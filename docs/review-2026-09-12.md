# Review and advancement — 12 September 2026

The starting checkout was clean at `fd47798`, with 116 passing tests. The user's shared parts table, new devices, SPICE interchange, Gerber/Excellon/centroid output, pours, routing controls and thermal energy-balance work were preserved.

## Findings addressed

- **Pour islands:** a rectangle used to join all same-net copper inside it, even across foreign-copper clearance cuts. A bounded graph now proves paths with exact capsule checks on every edge. Tests cover board-spanning cuts, intersecting cuts and enclosed islands. Narrow connections can remain unverified; the checker reports grid spacing and budget limits.
- **SPICE scope and polarity:** definition bodies and control commands no longer become top-level circuit devices; `.end` stops parsing. Unsupported PNP/PMOS or missing models are reported. Case variants of a net join correctly, malformed scopes fail, and every import note is accessible.
- **FFT sampling and amplitude:** the old record reduction could alias a high tone, and DC polluted the AC estimate. The scope now exposes a native-rate periodic-Hann spectrum with calibrated RMS dBV, explicit DC, sample count/rate/resolution, guarded harmonic ratio and CSV download.
- **Cross-view consistency:** solver net IDs now match schematic nets. Saved SI settings drive both detailed analysis and Research Lab. Copper reconciliation includes pours when schematic nets split or merge.
- **Thermal display:** removed the tenfold heat-source multiplier. Zero ambient is accepted, invalid parameters pause calculations, and modeled board temperature is distinguished from a device operating limit.
- **Fabrication preview:** rendering consumes generated Gerber commands, including negative-polarity pour cuts and actual mask openings. The entire package downloads as one ZIP; independent ZIP extraction verifies its 10 files. Preview aspect ratio, board fitting, scaled pointer coordinates and keyboard dismissal were checked.

## New workflow

Use **Board Setup** for the outline and six shared SI inputs. Save a project, recover it locally, or undo/redo setup changes. Use **SPICE Scope** for voltage waveforms and spectrum export. Use **Fab** for per-layer preview and the complete package ZIP. Existing routes and footprints retain their positions when the outline is resized; the board checker reports edge problems.

## Research and limits

[Research notes](research-notes.md) connect the changes to primary routing papers, KiCad, ngspice, Qucs and SciPy references. The implementation does not reproduce a routing benchmark. Footprints and behavioral semiconductor models remain illustrative. Pour checks conservatively prove some paths rather than performing general polygon-island extraction. Spectrum estimates retain off-bin window error and cannot detect pre-existing aliasing. SI and thermal results are preliminary models, not fabrication or device-safety approval.
